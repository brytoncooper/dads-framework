# Who let Gary back in?

Dad took Gary off the guest list. Ten minutes later Gary was in the kitchen
eating shrimp.

“Gary, who let you in?”

“Uncle Mike. I was on his list.”

There were two copies. Dad had crossed Gary off his. Uncle Mike was at the
front door with yesterday’s printout. The shrimp were very much from today.

Apps can have the same problem. Remove a workspace member in one database
and a separate permissions service still needs the news. An authorization
outbox keeps track of that delivery. Until it arrives, the door can give
yesterday’s answer.

If your records and permissions live in Postgres, Dads can remove the
membership and revoke access in one transaction. A fresh check on the
primary sees both changes together. There’s one guest list.
